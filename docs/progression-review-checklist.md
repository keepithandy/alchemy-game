# Customer Orders and Workshop Progression Review

Use this read-only playthrough checklist to decide whether customer orders and workshop upgrades feel distinct and understandable. Record observations first; do not treat this document as authorization to change rewards, prices, probabilities, recipes, or progression.

## Player-facing signals to observe

### Customer orders

- Can the player read the requested effect, minimum quality, reward, and deadline before deciding what to brew?
- Is the difference between an available order and an order without a matching potion clear in text and state?
- Does delivering an order clearly state the gold and reputation outcome?
- As orders refresh, are their requests distinct enough that the player can connect an order to a brewing goal?
- Is any unlock or availability change explained where the player sees it?

### Workshop upgrades

- Does each upgrade show its cost, installed/purchasable state, and concrete benefit before purchase?
- After purchase, can the player identify the upgrade as installed and understand what future brew or foraging result it affects?
- Do the four upgrades have noticeably different roles: grinding, heat control, sale value, and foraging yield?
- Is an unaffordable upgrade clearly distinguished from an already-installed upgrade?
